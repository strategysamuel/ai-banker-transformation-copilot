# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to providing a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free experience for everyone participating in this project, regardless of age, disability, ethnicity, gender identity or expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, religion, or sexual orientation.

## Our Standards

Examples of positive behaviour include:

- Being respectful, considerate, and constructive.
- Welcoming questions, differing viewpoints, and good-faith feedback.
- Giving and accepting actionable feedback professionally.
- Focusing on what is best for the project and its community.

Unacceptable behaviour includes harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, intimidation, doxxing, and publishing another person's private information without permission.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in project repositories, issues, pull requests, discussions, and other project spaces.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the repository maintainer through GitHub's private contact mechanisms. For security vulnerabilities, follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), not this process.

Reports will be reviewed as promptly and fairly as possible. The maintainer will respect the privacy of the reporter and anyone affected, to the extent possible.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, close contributions, or restrict participation for behaviour that violates this Code. Decisions will be proportionate to the circumstances.
