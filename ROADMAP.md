# Project Roadmap

This roadmap describes areas under consideration. It is not a delivery commitment. Priorities may change based on maintainer capacity, security findings, and community feedback.

## Current focus

- Keep authentication and server-side authorization tests reliable.
- Preserve Project Compass's fail-closed behaviour for unsupported or insufficiently grounded policy questions.
- Improve citation integrity, active-policy version checks, and human-verification signals.
- Keep synthetic demo data clearly separated from real institutional policy.
- Improve setup instructions and reproducible local validation.
- Review dependency and workflow security.

## Next areas to explore

- Contributor-friendly local development and test fixtures.
- More deterministic RAG evaluation cases, including adversarial and out-of-scope queries.
- Accessibility and usability improvements in the web interface.
- Architecture and threat-model documentation.
- Small, well-scoped starter issues for external contributors.

## Not in scope

- Executing live banking transactions or credit decisions.
- Supplying authoritative banking, legal, compliance, or regulatory advice.
- Ingesting confidential bank/customer data into the public demonstration corpus.
- Claiming compatibility with a bank's internal systems without a tested integration.

## Proposing work

Open a Feature Request with the problem, intended users, proposed outcome, security/privacy considerations, and how success could be tested. Please discuss large changes before investing in implementation.
